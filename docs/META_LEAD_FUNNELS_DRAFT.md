These campaign concepts are not approved for publishing and must not incur spend. Placeholder budget: **S$10/day per campaign**.

## Employer campaign

**Working name:** QY Workforce — Flexible Worker Deployment — Preview 01  
**Objective:** Lead generation  
**Destination:** https://qyworkforce.com/employers  
**CTA:** Request flexible workers  
**Primary text:** Need extra manpower without adding permanent headcount? QY Workforce helps employers deploy flexible workers across hospitality, F&B, cleaning, retail, promotions and events. Tell us the roles, headcount and timing you need.  
**Headline:** Flexible workers when you need them  
**Description:** Share your deployment needs and our team will follow up.  
**Audience hypothesis:** Singapore business decision-makers and managers in hospitality, F&B, retail, facilities, events and operations.  
**Form fields:** name, company, contact number, email, deployment timeline, worker roles/headcount, location, optional requirements, PDPA consent, optional WhatsApp follow-up consent.

## Worker campaign

**Working name:** QY Workforce — Gig Worker Recruitment — Preview 01  
**Objective:** Lead generation  
**Destination:** https://qyworkforce.com/workers  
**CTA:** Register for gig work  
**Primary text:** Looking for flexible shifts that fit your schedule? Register your interest for gig work across hospitality, F&B, cleaning, retail, promotions and events with QY Workforce.  
**Headline:** Flexible shifts. More choice.  
**Description:** Tell us what work you want and when you are available.  
**Audience hypothesis:** Singapore adults interested in part-time, temporary and gig work. Employment eligibility is verified separately and is never inferred from ad targeting.  
**Form fields:** name, contact number, email, work interests/roles, availability, preferred locations, optional notes, PDPA consent, optional WhatsApp follow-up consent.
